Fresh Pasta Dough

  • Time: About 45mins
  • Rating: 5
  • Notes: Read Community notes

Creating fresh homemade pasta dough is a therapeutic culinary ritual that transforms simple pantry staples into a canvas for incredible meals. The process begins by mounding high-quality flour—ideally finely ground "00" flour or a mix with semolina—directly onto a clean work surface and clearing a wide well in the center to resemble a miniature volcano. Into this crater, you crack fresh, vibrant eggs, often adding an extra splash of olive oil or a pinch of salt. Using a fork, you gently beat the eggs in the center and slowly start whisking in the flour from the inner walls of the well, bit by bit, until a thick, custard-like paste forms. Once the mixture becomes too thick for the fork, you discard it and use your hands or a bench scraper to bring the remaining loose flour together into a shaggy, rough ball. The magic happens during the kneading phase: using the heel of your hand, you firmly push the dough forward, fold it back, and rotate, repeating this rhythmic motion for about 8 to 10 minutes. As you knead, the initial dry, stubborn texture yields to the development of gluten, gradually transforming into a silky, smooth, and elastic dough that springs back lightly when poked. Finally, the dough is wrapped tightly in plastic wrap and left to rest at room temperature for at least 30 minutes, a crucial step that relaxes the gluten and ensures it will roll out beautifully into tender sheets of pasta.

Preparation

  1. Mound the flour in a wide mixing bowl
  2. Beat Together eggs
  3. Mix eggs with flour
  4. Transfer to counter surface and knead
  5. Rest dough for 30mins
